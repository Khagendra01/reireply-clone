Humanizer 2.0
The Humanizer replaces standard drip timing with randomized delays between actions in your workflow. Instead of every message going out at fixed intervals, you set a time range (for example, 60–240 seconds), and each contact is assigned a random delay before the next email, text, or call step.
Why it’s vital for cold outreach:

Carriers monitor sending patterns, especially timing footprints. Fixed drip intervals create obvious automation signals. The Humanizer removes that footprint by making your outreach timing look natural and unpredictable.
When combined with spintax (message variation), it prevents sending the exact same message at the exact same time, dramatically improving deliverability and helping keep your account safe.
